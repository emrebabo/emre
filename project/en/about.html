About ASM

ASM d.o.o. is a specialized operational service partner for Identity and Verification platforms operating in regulated environments. We support clients in the structured execution of sensitive identification processes within existing systems – fully system-integrated, SLA-based and operationally stable.

Development & Specialization

  • 2016: Foundation with structured SLA operations
  • 2018–2020: Documented process models & role governance
  • 2021–2023: Specialized identity case handling in regulated environments
  • Since 2024: Full specialization in Identity & Verification Operations

Operational Governance

  • Role-based access models
  • Defined escalation and review structures
  • No parallel tools or external data storage
  • System-integrated KPI/SLA performance governance

Operational Reality

Founded in 2016, ASM evolved through documented process models, role governance, escalation structures and quality controls anchored fully inside client systems. Since 2024, ASM has consistently specialized in Identity and Verification Operations with audit-ready documentation and controlled SLA management. Our teams handle document and identity checks, remote identification, escalation-driven case handling and SLA-critical volume management while maintaining strict process integrity and confidentiality.
