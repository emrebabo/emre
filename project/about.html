🇬🇧 About ASM

ASM d.o.o. is a specialized operational service partner for Identity and Verification platforms operating in regulated environments. We support clients in the structured execution of sensitive identification processes within existing systems – fully system-integrated, SLA-based and operationally stable.

Development & Specialization

Founded in 2016 for structured SLA-based operations. 2018–2020 introduced documented process models, roles, escalation and quality reviews in client systems. 2021–2023 focused on regulated identity environments and specialized case-handling. Since 2024, ASM is fully specialized in Identity and Verification Operations with audit-ready documentation and controlled SLA management.

Operational Reality

  • Document and identity verification processes
  • Remote identification workflows
  • Structured case handling with defined escalation paths
  • SLA-critical volume management
  • Compliance-oriented process documentation

Operational Governance

Defined team structures, lead functions and established quality reviews are fully integrated into client systems. No parallel tools or external data storage are used.

Security & Responsibility

Role-based access, documented process flows and operational integrity inside client systems.

Our Role

ASM does not develop its own software and does not assume regulatory end decisions. We act as a stable extension of internal identity operations.

Structure & Location

Headquartered in Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina. Teams operate in a structured, multilingual and compliance-oriented model.

🇩🇪 Über ASM

ASM d.o.o. ist ein spezialisierter operativer Service-Partner für Identity- und Verification-Plattformen im regulierten Umfeld. Wir unterstützen Auftraggeber bei der strukturierten Umsetzung sensibler Identifikationsprozesse innerhalb bestehender Kundensysteme – systemintegriert, SLA-basiert und operativ stabil.

Entwicklung & Spezialisierung: 2016 Gründung mit Fokus auf strukturierte SLA-Operations; 2018–2020 dokumentierte Prozessmodelle, Rollen- und Eskalationsstrukturen; 2021–2023 Spezialisierung auf identitätsnahe Prozesse; seit 2024 konsequente Spezialisierung auf Identity- und Verification-Operations mit auditfähiger Dokumentation und kontrollierter SLA-Steuerung.

Operative Realität: Dokumenten- und Identitätsprüfungen, Remote-Identifikationsprozesse, strukturierte Fallbearbeitung, SLA-kritische Volumensteuerung, compliance-nahe Prozessdokumentation.

Governance: Definierte Teamstrukturen, Lead-Funktionen, Qualitätsprüfungen innerhalb der Kundensysteme; keine parallelen Tools oder externen Datenspeicher.

🇧🇦 O ASM-u

ASM d.o.o. je specijalizovani operativni partner za Identity i Verification platforme u regulisanom okruženju. Podržavamo klijente u strukturiranoj realizaciji osjetljivih identifikacijskih procesa unutar postojećih sistema – sistemski integrisano, uz SLA standarde i operativnu stabilnost.

Razvoj i specijalizacija: osnivanje 2016, period 2018–2020 dokumentovani procesi i eskalacije, 2021–2023 fokus na regulisana identity okruženja, od 2024 potpuna specijalizacija uz audit-ready dokumentovanje i kontrolisano SLA upravljanje.

Operativna realnost: provjera dokumenata i identiteta, udaljena identifikacija, strukturirana obrada slučajeva, SLA upravljanje obimom, regulatorno usklađeno dokumentovanje procesa.
